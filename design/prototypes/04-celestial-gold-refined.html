Twelve rashis of 30°, twenty-seven nakshatras of 13°20′. The gold bead is the Moon: it moves. The divisions never do.

A nakshatra is not a body. It is a fixed band of the sidereal zodiac that the Moon crosses in about a day — roughly 13°20′ at its average speed. So Dhanishta is right now; past 306°40′ the Moon is in Shatabhisha, then P. Bhadrapada, U. Bhadrapada, Revati, and on into Aquarius. “Pada 2 of Capricorn” is shorthand: the pada belongs to Dhanishta, and Capricorn is the rashi it falls inside.
